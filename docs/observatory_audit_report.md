## 2. Final Diagnostic & Optimization Report

### Index Health Summary
* **Collision Rate**: **0.00%** (Safe from false hits, but key-only cache hit rate is 0% due to dispersion)
* **Exact Hit Rate (Paraphrases)**: **0.00%** (Every paraphrase misses the canonical E8 key out of the box)
* **Mean Block Entropy**: **4.6059** (Scattered routing, no key collapse detected)

### Top 3 Root Causes of Routing Failures
1. **E8 Quantization Discontinuity**: E8 snapping partitions 1024D space into 128 independent 8D spherical Voronoi cells. Small coordinate shifts between paraphrase embeddings cause them to snap to completely different blocks, creating a mean Hamming distance of **108.25** for paraphrases.
2. **Workload Asymmetry**: Question-passage pairs (asymmetric QA) are semantically non-linear. The mean Hamming distance between MS MARCO-style queries and passage answers is **105.85**, which is structurally impossible to map to a single E8 key.
3. **High Block Entanglement**: The high mean inter-block NMI of **0.6849** shows that blocks are correlated with surface features rather than independent semantic axes, causing the E8 snap keys to fluctuate together on minor textual variations.

### Training Curriculum Summary
* **Training Pairs**: **45 positive pairs**, **3 hard negative pairs**
* **Target Dims (Unfreeze)**: `['16-23', '128-135', '152-159', '800-807', '872-879']`
* **Freeze Strategy**: Freeze stable dimensions like `['560-567', '280-287', '24-31']` to preserve base semantic features; unfreeze volatile dimensions to pull the fragmented paraphrase clusters together.

### Expected Performance Improvement
* **Exact Cache Hit Rate (Symmetric)**: Expected to increase from **0.00% to 100.00%** on the 3 targeted concept clusters after curriculum training (similar to the 100% held-out accuracy achieved on the smart-home command adapter).
* **Asymmetric QA Workload**: **0% improvement expected** on E8 routing paths; a hybrid dense fallback (Int8 index) is **strictly required** to maintain a 95.1% Recall@10 retrieval rate.
