What this measures: For each domain, paraphrase prompts are routed to a pre-approved cached answer (12 intents each). Recall is the fraction of paraphrase queries correctly routed. FP rate is wrong routes on hard near-miss queries — semantically adjacent prompts that belong to a different intent. Cache hit rate simulates the fraction of real traffic served from cache. Latency is lookup overhead only (excludes encoding).
